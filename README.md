# Jal Drishti

**Jal Drishti** is an AI-powered urban resilience navigation platform designed to protect commuters, gig workers, and city residents from extreme weather hazards. By combining crowdsourced computer vision analysis with geospatial environmental intelligence, Jal Drishti enables dynamic, safety-first street navigation across extreme wet and dry seasons.

---

## Key Features

### 🌧️ Monsoon Mode (Water)
* **Crowdsourced Visual Reporting:** Users capture and upload street-level photos of waterlogged roads.
* **AI Passability Grading:** Computer vision models analyze image depth and water levels to classify passability into three tiers:
  * `Safe`
  * `Two-Wheeler Caution`
  * `Blocked`
* **Real-Time Hazard Heatmap:** Dynamically plots submerged areas and open drain choke points.
* **Smart Rerouting:** Automatically reroutes users away from waterlogged streets and vehicle-damaging hazards.

### ☀️ Heatwave Mode (Heat)
* **Canopy & Thermal Mapping:** Ranks street segments based on tree canopy shade and asphalt surface temperature (mitigating 44°C+ heat risks).
* **Cool Route Navigation:** Provides an alternate "Cool Route" that prioritizes shaded streets, water refill kiosks, and cooling zones over direct, exposed routes.
* **Vulnerable Commuter Protection:** Built specifically for pedestrians, delivery riders, and two-wheeler commuters exposed to prolonged heat stress.
